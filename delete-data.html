Delete your Timberhaven data

Timberhaven: Idle Lumberjack, by LiZenApps, has no online sign-in. Each "Account" is a save on your device.

Delete in the game

  1. Open the game and tap the Settings gear.
  2. Tap Delete account and data, then Delete.
  3. Copy the code shown on screen if you also want server records deleted.

This deletes that account's save on your device and its iCloud backup, and resets this device's analytics identifiers and unsent crash reports.

Delete analytics records on our servers

Email the code to lizenapps@gmail.com. We submit a deletion request to Google Analytics for Firebase within 30 days. If you no longer have the code, we cannot link records to you, because we do not store your name or email with them.

What is kept

  • Crash reports already sent: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Advertising data: held by Google AdMob under Google's retention rules. We cannot delete it. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Purchase records: held by Google Play and Apple, as the law requires. Manage them in your store account.
  • Game Center scores on iOS: held by Apple under your Apple account.
